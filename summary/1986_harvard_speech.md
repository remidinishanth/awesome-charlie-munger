**The Carson Prescriptions for Misery**

1. **Ingesting Chemicals**: Using alcohol or drugs to alter your mood or perception.

2. **Envy**: Letting the success of others rot your own satisfaction.

3. **Resentment**: Holding onto bitterness and anger toward others.

**The Munger Prescriptions for Misery**

1. **Be Unreliable**: Do not faithfully do what you have engaged to do. This one habit can cancel out all other virtues.

2. **Learn Only from Your Own Mistakes**: Avoid "vicarious wisdom." Refuse to learn from the best work and the worst failures of those who came before you.

3. **Go Down and Stay Down**: When life hits you with a severe reversal (failure), give up immediately and never get back up.

4. Ignore "The Rustic's" Wisdom (Non-Inversion): Fail to look at problems backward. Ignore the advice to "Invert, always invert" when trying to solve complex life problems.
